# Executive Summary: Shipping Route Efficiency Analysis

**Prepared for:** Nassau Candy Distributor Board & Logistics Stakeholders  
**Scope:** 10,194 nationwide confectionery shipments from 5 factory locations to customers in 48 states.

---

## Stakeholder Headline Findings (Plain Language)

1. **Systemic Order-to-Shipment Transit Lag**
   The average shipping lead time across Nassau Candy's network is **1,320.84 days (approx. 3.6 years)**. This extreme delay points to a massive, systemic bottleneck in post-production inventory handling or order-to-shipping processing rather than typical road carriage delays.

2. **The "Expedited Shipping Paradox" (Logistics Failure)**
   Expedited orders (Same Day and First Class) take **19 to 24 days longer** to arrive than Standard Class shipments. Premium shipping options are not being prioritized in the packing and dispatch queue, resulting in customers paying express fees for slower service.

3. **High-Volume Congestion Bottlenecks**
   Out of 196 active routes, **54 routes are flagged as congestion hotspots**. Key lanes, such as `Wicked Choccy's ➔ New Mexico` and `Lot's O' Nuts ➔ Iowa`, suffer from high volumes and severe transit times, indicating major loading dock and regional carrier shortages.

4. **West Coast and Northern Tier Disproportionate Delays**
   Shipments originating from `Sugar Shack` (Minnesota) and destined for the Northeast (`Sugar Shack ➔ Connecticut` at 1,641 days) or West Coast represent the slowest lanes in the network. The remote location of these plants exacerbates long-haul transit drag.

## Prioritized Strategic Route Recommendations

| Action | Route/Shipping Mode | Rationale | Owner | Next Step |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enforce priority packing & dispatch FIFO** | Expedited Shipping (First Class & Same Day) | Express shipments take longer than Standard Class (1,338d vs 1,314d). | VP of Warehouse Operations | Establish a dedicated priority lane in the packing zone and automate express alerts in the ERP. |
| **Transit audit & regional 3PL contract** | Wicked Choccy's (Savannah) ➔ New Mexico | High-volume lane (17 orders) suffering from 64.7% delay frequency. | Director of Carrier Relations | Partner with a Southwest regional 3PL carrier to manage long-haul transit from Savannah. |
| **Route diversion & hub consolidation** | Lot's O' Nuts (Arizona) ➔ Iowa | Major Midwest artery (16 orders) with 56.2% delay rate. | Logistics Planning Manager | Divert Iowa shipments through a secondary sorting hub in Chicago to bypass local bottlenecks. |
| **SKU redistribution** | Sugar Shack (Minnesota) ➔ Northeast (NJ / CT) | Sugar Shack to Northeast routes represent our slowest lanes (1,642d average). | Director of Supply Chain | Stock high-demand sugar SKUs at Illinois (Secret Factory) to fulfill Northeast orders. |
| **Expedited surcharge freeze** | Same Day / First Class options | Nassau Candy is charging premium rates for sub-standard delivery times. | VP of Commercial Sales | Freeze express shipping surcharges for customers on lanes exceeding 1,300 days until queue logic is fixed. |

## Critical Next Steps & Action Plan

- **Immediate (Weeks 1-2):**
  - **VP of Warehouse Operations** to audit the packaging floor and ensure Same Day/First Class orders are physically prioritized on the sorting line.
  - **VP of Commercial Sales** to identify accounts affected by the "Expedited Paradox" and issue credits or shipping refunds to protect customer relationships.
- **Mid-Term (Month 1):**
  - **Director of Supply Chain** to analyze the feasibility of warehousing Illinois-manufactured products in a Midwest fulfillment center to optimize standard shipping lanes.
  - **Logistics Planning Manager** to renegotiate carrier contracts for the 54 identified bottleneck lanes, incorporating strict SLA penalties for delays exceeding 1,400 days.
- **Long-Term (Quarter 1):**
  - Implement a real-time warehouse management system (WMS) that dynamically schedules carrier pick-ups based on order urgency.
