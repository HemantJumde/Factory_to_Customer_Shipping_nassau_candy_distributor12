# Executive Summary: Product Line Profitability & Margin Performance

**Prepared for:** Nassau Candy Distributor C-Suite Executive Team  
**Scope:** 10,194 Order Transactions across 15 Product SKUs, 3 Divisions, and 5 Manufacturing Facilities.

---

## C-Suite Headline Findings (Plain Language)

1. **Extreme Cocoa Dependency (Product Concentration Risk)**
   A mere five products (all in the Chocolate division) generate **95.1% of Nassau Candy's total gross profit** ($88,824.62 out of $93,442.80). Any production stoppage, supply chain delay, or raw cocoa price spike at Wicked Choccy's (Savannah, Georgia) or Lot's O' Nuts (Casa Grande, Arizona) directly threatens company viability.

2. **Severe Underperformance of 'Other' Candy Portfolio**
   While the Chocolate division operates at a highly profitable **67.45% gross margin**, the Other division is underperforming relative to its sales share. Other represents **6.8% of revenue but only 4.6% of gross profit** due to significant margin compression, particularly within the Kazookles product line.

3. **Critical Margin Bleed on Kazookles**
   `Kazookles` is a high-volume product generating $1,205.75 in sales but returns a net gross profit of just **$92.75 (a compressed 7.69% gross margin)**. Confectionery manufacturing costs consume **92.31 cents of every dollar sold**, making it a Critical Risk item.

4. **Under-utilized Sugar Distribution Capacity**
   The Sugar division achieves a very healthy **66.61% gross margin** but generates only **$427.48 in total revenue** (0.3% of profit contribution) across seven active SKUs. This represents extreme under-utilization of Nassau Candy's marketing and warehouse channels.
