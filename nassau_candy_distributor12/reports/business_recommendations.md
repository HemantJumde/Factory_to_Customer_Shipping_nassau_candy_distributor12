## 2. Priority Strategic Initiatives

### Initiative A: Immediate Remediation of `Kazookles` (Critical Risk)
- **Problem:** `Kazookles` generates $1,205.75 in revenue but costs $1,113.00 to manufacture (**92.31% cost ratio**), returning a non-viable 7.69% gross margin.
- **Action Plan:**
  1. **Price Adjustment:** Implement an immediate **35% price increase** (raising unit wholesale price from $3.25 to $4.39).
  2. **Cost Audit:** Audit raw manufacturing overhead at **The Other Factory** (Memphis, TN).
  3. **Expected Impact:** Increases `Kazookles` gross margin from 7.69% to **31.6%**, generating **+$385.00** in incremental gross profit.

### Initiative B: Protect Supply Chain & Manufacturing at Wicked Choccy's & Lot's O' Nuts
- **Problem:** 95.06% of company gross profit originates from 5 Chocolate products manufactured at **Wicked Choccy's** (Savannah, GA) and **Lot's O' Nuts** (Casa Grande, AZ).
- **Action Plan:**
  1. Establish long-term forward supply contracts for cocoa butter, sugar, and milk powder to lock in raw input costs.
  2. Implement preventive maintenance schedules at Savannah and Casa Grande plants to guarantee 99.5%+ uptime.

### Initiative C: Commercial Cross-Selling for High-Margin "Grow" Products
- **Problem:** `Everlasting Gobstopper` (80.0% margin) and `Hair Toffee` (77.8% margin) have outstanding profitability but minimal distribution volume ($130 and $76.50 sales).
- **Action Plan:**
  1. Create a **"Confectionery Favorites"** promotional bundle combining core Wonka Chocolate bars with Gobstoppers and Hair Toffee.
  2. Offer retail partners a 5% discount on chocolate orders if they add 2 display boxes of Sugar division products.
  3. **Expected Impact:** Expanding sales volume by 5x on these SKUs yields **+$800.00** in high-margin gross profit.

### Initiative D: SKU Rationalization & Handling Overhead Reduction
- **Problem:** 4 Sugar SKUs (`Fun Dip`, `Nerds`, `SweeTARTS`, `Fizzy Lifting Drinks`) collectively generate only **$87.75 gross profit** across 23 orders.
- **Action Plan:**
  1. Establish a **Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)** of 50 units per SKU for small sugar lines.
  2. Transition slow-moving sugar SKUs to seasonal or custom-pack distribution only.
